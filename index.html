Logo Design Case Study | EDC VIT

Vishwapreneur 13 — Transcending Through Ordinary

The Context

Vishwapreneur is Pune’s biggest E-Summit, bringing together people from diverse domains to share real journeys, lessons and experiences. The 13th edition explored Alchemy, the transformation of something ordinary into something valuable. This felt especially relevant as EDC VIIT merged with EDC VIT, bringing its own phase of uncertainty, restructuring and growth.

The Concept

I interpreted Alchemy as a journey of transformation, both for the team and the audience. The logo combines the V of Vishwapreneur with a parachute, symbolising:

  • Taking the leap beyond the familiar
  • Flying higher through ambition and ideas
  • Opening yourself to global opportunities
  • Transforming from ordinary to extraordinary
Visual Language
  • Orange: Inspired by alchemy’s transformation of base metals into gold, reinterpreted into a youthful, energetic tone.
  • Gradient: Moves light → dark → light → dark, representing the non-linear journey of personal growth. Progress isn't always a straight line.
  • Overall Form: A balance of corporate professionalism + student ambition, reflecting the people and energy behind Vishwapreneur.
My Design Approach

Rather than choosing Alchemy because it simply looked good, I questioned what the team was experiencing, what the audience should feel, and what story the event needed to tell. The final identity connected two transformations: Our transformation as a team + the audience's transformation through the event.

The Brief

Verge is a German-inspired, non-alcoholic beverage designed primarily for seniors who want something refreshing, not overly sweet, familiar yet distinctive, and luxurious.

The Idea

The brand brings together nostalgia, culture and modern indulgence, creating a drink that lets seniors reconnect with familiar memories while remaining relevant to younger generations.

Design Direction
  • • Glass bottles: Familiar packaging inspired by traditional beverages, creating a sense of nostalgia and trust.
  • • German architectural imagery: References historic German buildings and creates a connection to heritage and culture.
  • • Classic italic typography: Adds elegance, sophistication and a timeless character.
  • • Floral visual language: Gives the brand a softer, refreshing summer personality.
  • • Rich, restrained palette: Positions Verge as premium without feeling inaccessible.
The Experience

A taste of nostalgia, redesigned for today. It gives older audiences a connection to the past, while allowing younger audiences to discover and celebrate a piece of that culture in a new way.
